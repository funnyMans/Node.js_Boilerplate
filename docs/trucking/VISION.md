# Vision and boundaries

## Purpose

Build a shared working platform that helps the people involved in trucking
work see what they are responsible for, make or request the decisions within
their authority, and hand work to the right person without losing ownership.

The immediate problem is coordination: a customer request becomes a
serviceable load, a responsible person finds capacity, an authorized
supervisor confirms the plan, and the driver or carrier carries it out.
Relevant exceptions must reach someone who can respond.

## Reference company

Use a US trucking company with its own fleet and brokerage as the example:
roughly 50–100 trucks, drivers, brokers, dispatchers, area supervisors, and
transportation leadership. The numbers are illustrative, not product limits.
The working geography is LA, West, Central, and East; LA is its own area.

The platform should serve each participant's authorized work. That does not
mean combining every company department or every logistics responsibility
into one product.

## In scope for this discussion

- Actors, responsibilities, permissions, and decision authority.
- Who owns a load decision and how dispatchers, brokers, supervisors,
  drivers, and carriers coordinate it.
- Sourcing and assignment priorities, availability, and exception handoffs.
- The minimum operational facts needed by participants to do that work.

## Deliberately deferred

- Analytics, forecasts, scorecards, performance ranking, and secondary use of
  collected data.
- CRM/TMS/ERP boundaries, integrations, retention policy, and data platforms.
- Whether users share one application, use role-focused applications, or
  access separate services through APIs.
- A complete product for every load type, customer, department, or partner.

These exclusions concern business analytics and use of operational data.
Engineering telemetry needed to keep the platform healthy belongs to the
separate technical architecture discussion.

Those topics can be revisited when an actual workflow requires them. For now,
the smallest useful goal is to make responsibilities and handoffs clear.
